    How to use this site

    1. Choose a topic above, or type a word in the search box (for example: water, helmet, vote).
    2. Use the language filter for English, हिन्दी or ਪੰਜਾਬੀ.
    3. Tap Copy to paste a slogan into a chart, a banner design or a message, WhatsApp to send it to your class group, or Save to keep a shortlist on this device.
    4. Want to write your own? Read How to write a slogan, with poster tips for competitions.

    Whose slogans are these?

    Most slogans on this site were written by us for this site. They are free to use on your poster, banner, rally or school project.

    Some slogans are so widely used in India that no topic feels complete without them, such as "जल है तो कल है" or "रक्तदान, महादान". We did not write those. On each page they are kept apart under the heading In common use (not written by us), so you always know which is which.

    The famous slogans of the freedom movement are on their own page, Historic slogans, each with the person who gave it and the year.

    Questions people ask

    Can I use these slogans in a competition or project?

    Yes. They are free to use on posters, charts, banners and in speeches. If a competition asks for a self-written slogan, use ours for ideas and write your own with the help of our guide.

    Did you write all the slogans?

    We wrote most of them. Well-known public slogans are listed separately on each page under "In common use (not written by us)". Historic slogans are on their own page with the person named.

    Are there slogans in Hindi and Punjabi?

    Yes. Most topics have rhyming slogans in हिन्दी and many in ਪੰਜਾਬੀ. See the Hindi and Punjabi pages for all of them together.

    Can I use a business tagline from this site for my shop?

    You can, but first search online to make sure another business near you, or a registered brand, does not already use the same words. We cannot check that for you.

    Who gave the slogan "Jai Jawan Jai Kisan"?

    Lal Bahadur Shastri, in 1965. More such answers are on the Historic slogans page.
