Save electricity and fuel slogans

National Energy Conservation Day is observed in India on 14 December. These lines also work on the small sticker next to a switchboard.

  • Lights off, savings on.

  • An empty room needs no fan.

  • Save a unit today. It adds up by the month.

  • Sunlight is free. Open the curtains.

  • Unplug what you are not using.

  • Take the stairs. Save power. Gain health.

  • Engine off at the red light.

  • Use what you need. Save what you can.

  • The cheapest unit of electricity is the one you never use.

  • The sun charges nothing. Use it till evening.

Where these slogans work

Energy slogans are reminders more than rally cries. Their place is beside the switchboard, on the classroom door, near the lift, on the geyser, by the office printer and at the petrol pump queue. A small sticker read fifty times a day does more than a banner read once.

Write your own

Catch the moment of waste. The fan turning in an empty room. The charger left on. The engine running at a red light. The fridge door held open while someone decides. Then give the two-second cure: switch, unplug, turn off, close. A slogan that names the moment is obeyed, because the reader recognises himself.

  • English pairs: light and bright, switch and rich, power and hour, save and behave, off and enough.
  • Hindi pairs: बिजली and असली, बचाओ and जलाओ, बत्ती and मस्ती, बंद and आनंद, ऊर्जा and पुर्ज़ा.

Ideas for a class or office

  • Make a "last one out" rule and paint it on the door: the last person switches everything off.
  • Appoint an energy monitor for the week and let the class write that week's slogan.
  • Put the slogan on the bill board: pin up last month's bill beside it.

What to avoid

  • Promising exact savings in rupees or units. They depend on the appliance and the tariff.
  • Slogans that ask people to sit in the dark or the heat. Ask for care, not hardship.

Using these slogans

  • A reminder works best at the point of action: near the switch, the lift, the AC remote.
