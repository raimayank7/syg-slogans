Farmer and Kisan Diwas slogans

Kisan Diwas, the National Farmers' Day, is observed in India on 23 December, the birthday of former Prime Minister Chaudhary Charan Singh. These slogans thank the people who grow our food.

English

  • Before your breakfast, a farmer had already done half a day's work.

  • The mandi is full because someone's nights were sleepless.

  • Thank a farmer three times a day.

  • Every roti began in someone's field.

  • The hands that feed us deserve our respect.

  • He reads the sky the way we read screens.

  • Do not waste what took a season to grow.

  • Soil on the hands, food on the nation's table.

  • Buy fairly. Someone's year depends on it.

  • The farmer's holiday is the day after the harvest.

Where these slogans are used

Kisan Diwas programmes, harvest festival functions such as Baisakhi, Pongal and Makar Sankranti, agricultural fairs, school charts and speeches. Many families who now live in cities are one generation away from a farm, so these lines speak to more people than might be expected.

Write your own

Honour the work, and be exact about it. The farmer in your slogan wakes before the sun, watches the sky, waters at night when the power comes, and waits half a year for one harvest. A single true detail, such as the tubewell, the trolley, the mandi or the folded hands at sowing time, carries more respect than large words about "the backbone of the nation". Link the farm to the reader's own plate: every roti began in a field.

  • English pairs: sow and grow, field and yield, grain and rain, soil and toil, harvest and honest.
  • Hindi pairs: किसान and भगवान, खेत and रेत, अन्नदाता and विधाता, फ़सल and असल, पसीना and नगीना.
  • Punjabi pairs: ਕਿਸਾਨ and ਸ਼ਾਨ, ਖੇਤ and ਹੇਤ, ਫ਼ਸਲ and ਨਸਲ, ਮਿੱਟੀ and ਚਿੱਠੀ.

A historic slogan

The best known farmer slogan in India, "Jai Jawan Jai Kisan", was given by Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri in 1965. It is listed with other historic slogans on the historic slogans page. Use it as it is, with his name.

What to avoid

  • Party politics, protests and policy debates in school work. Thank the farmer, and leave the arguments to another forum.
  • Prices, figures and scheme details written from memory.
  • Pity. Farmers are skilled people who deserve respect and a fair deal, not sympathy.

Using these slogans

  • At a school function, invite a farmer to speak for five minutes. No slogan will do as much.

Questions

When is Kisan Diwas?

On 23 December every year, the birthday of Chaudhary Charan Singh.
