Road safety slogans

For Road Safety Week, school rallies, traffic police campaigns and the back of an auto. Short lines on helmets, seat belts, speed and phones.

Where road slogans are used

Road Safety Week rallies, school gate banners, the back of buses, autos and trucks, driving school walls, and the parking area of offices and societies. The sharpest place of all is the family vehicle: a child's handmade sticker on the dashboard has slowed many a parent down.

Write your own

Road slogans work when they remind the driver of someone waiting. Speed, helmets and phones are the subjects, but the feeling is always home. Choose one behaviour: helmet, seat belt, speed, phone, drink, signal, zebra crossing, or wrong side. Then say who it protects. Keep it short enough to read in the two seconds a driver has.

  • English pairs: speed and bleed, belt and felt, late and fate, rule and cool, wait and gate.
  • Hindi pairs: रफ़्तार and परिवार, हेलमेट and सेफ़, जल्दी and ग़लती, नियम and संयम, सावधानी and ज़िंदगानी.
  • Punjabi pairs: ਰਫ਼ਤਾਰ and ਪਰਿਵਾਰ, ਕਾਹਲੀ and ਖ਼ਾਲੀ, ਸਬਰ and ਖ਼ਬਰ.

For young children

Children are passengers and pedestrians, so give them slogans about their own part: holding a hand while crossing, looking both ways, sitting still in the school van, wearing a helmet on a parent's two-wheeler. Slogans beginning with "Papa" or "Mummy" are the most effective road safety campaign ever invented.

What to avoid

  • Gory drawings. They are remembered for the wrong reason, and many schools do not allow them.
  • Quoting fines, penalties and accident figures unless you have read them in an official source this year. They change.
  • Mocking any kind of driver. Everyone on the road has to get home.

Using these slogans

  • The best road slogan names one action: wear, slow, stop, wait.
  • For children's posters, use "I" and "my": "My helmet, my rule."
