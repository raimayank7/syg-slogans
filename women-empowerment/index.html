Women empowerment and Women's Day slogans

International Women's Day is observed on 8 March every year. These slogans are about equal chances, equal respect and equal say, at home, at school and at work.

English

  • Equal work. Equal pay. Equal say.

  • She does not need a pedestal. She needs a fair chance.

  • Educate a girl, and she will not need rescuing.

  • Respect is not a gift for one day in March.

  • Where she goes is her decision.

  • Strong women are raised by families that listen.

  • Share the housework. Share the future.

  • A daughter at the wheel, a nation on the move.

  • Ask her opinion. Then take it seriously.

  • Safety is her right, not her responsibility alone.

  • Boys who respect at home, men who respect everywhere.

  • She can, so let her.

  • Count her in, from the first meeting.

Where these slogans are used

Women's Day programmes in schools, colleges and offices, self-help group meetings, rallies, essay and speech competitions, and posters. They also suit the farewell of a woman colleague or the felicitation of a girl who has topped, where a line on a banner means more than a bouquet.

Write your own

The strongest lines on this subject are not about worship or pity. Calling a woman a goddess does not get her a seat at the table, and calling her weak is simply untrue. Write about fairness in ordinary things: the same schooling, the same pay for the same work, a share in decisions, safety on the road, housework shared at home. Lines addressed to men and boys are among the most useful, because respect is everybody's work.

  • English pairs: equal and sequel, voice and choice, rise and wise, she and free, respect and expect.
  • Hindi pairs: नारी and भारी, बराबर and घर घर, सम्मान and पहचान, शक्ति and भक्ति, हक़ and बेशक.
  • Punjabi pairs: ਔਰਤ and ਤਾਕਤ, ਮਾਣ and ਸ਼ਾਨ, ਬਰਾਬਰ and ਘਰ ਘਰ.

Three poster ideas

  • A weighing scale with a boy and a girl at the same level, and the slogan across the beam.
  • A row of women at work as a pilot, farmer, doctor, mechanic, sarpanch and teacher, with one line beneath.
  • A kitchen with the whole family cooking, for a slogan on sharing work at home.

What to avoid

  • Lines that praise women only for sacrifice. The point is that sacrifice should not be demanded.
  • Lines that insult men. Equality has no enemy.
  • Laws, sections and helpline numbers written from memory. Take them from an official source on the day, or leave them out.

Using these slogans

  • On a school poster, show girls and boys doing the same thing side by side.

Questions

When is International Women's Day?

On 8 March every year.

Can these slogans be used for a speech?

Yes. Open or close the speech with one line, and say it slowly.
