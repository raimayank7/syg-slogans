School election and head boy, head girl slogans

For student council campaigns. Put your own name where it says [Name]. The best campaign slogans promise something small and real.

English

  • Vote [Name]: fewer speeches, more work.

  • A captain who listens. Vote [Name].

  • Your voice, my job. [Name] for head girl.

  • I will not promise the moon. I will fix the water cooler. Vote [Name].

  • Ready to serve, not to rule.

  • Pick the one who shows up. Pick [Name].

  • Small promises, kept. [Name] for house captain.

  • For every bench, front and back. Vote [Name].

  • Less talk, more action, full attendance.

  • Your ideas plus my effort. [Name] for council.

  • One school, one team. Let us begin. Vote [Name].

हिन्दी

  • वादे कम, काम ज़्यादा। [नाम] को वोट दें।

  • आपकी बात, मेरी ज़िम्मेदारी।

  • जो सुने सबकी, वही कप्तान। [नाम] को चुनें।

  • छोटा वादा, पक्का इरादा।

  • कुर्सी के लिए नहीं, सेवा के लिए।

  • हर कक्षा की आवाज़, [नाम] के साथ।

  • चुनिए उसे जो हमेशा साथ खड़ा रहे।

Nothing matches. Try a shorter word, or choose All.

Using these slogans

  • Rhyme your name if you can. If you cannot, promise one thing clearly.
  • Never mock the other candidate. Schools remember, and so do voters.
