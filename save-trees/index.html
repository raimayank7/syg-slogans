Save trees and plantation slogans

For plantation drives, Van Mahotsav week (the first week of July in India) and any poster with a green crayon on it.

  • Plant a tree. Grow a future.

  • One student, one sapling.

  • Trees give shade to people they will never meet.

  • Cut a tree, cut your own breath.

  • Green today, alive tomorrow.

  • Do not just plant it. Raise it.

  • More trees, more birds, more rain.

  • A tree is a gift that keeps growing.

  • Hug a tree. It has been holding the soil for you.

  • Shade is free. Plant some.

  • Let the next generation climb trees, not just read about them.

  • Plant it today. Sit under it someday.

  • A tree asks for water for two summers, then gives for a hundred.

  • One child, one tree, one promise.

  • Shade is a gift someone planted years ago.

  • Water it through two summers. It will shade you for forty.

Where these slogans are used

Plantation drives, Van Mahotsav week, eco-club boards, the tree guard around a new sapling, and the banner at a society or village planting day. A slogan tied to a real sapling, with the name of the child or class that planted it, is remembered far longer than one on a chart.

Write your own tree slogan

The strongest tree slogans say what a tree gives: shade, fruit, air, rain, a home for birds, a swing in summer. Choose one gift and one promise. "This neem gives shade. I give it water." The promise is the part most slogans forget, and it is the part that keeps the sapling alive after the photograph is taken.

  • English pairs: tree and free, green and clean, plant and grant, shade and made, root and fruit.
  • Hindi pairs: पेड़ and ढेर, हरियाली and ख़ुशहाली, लगाओ and बचाओ, छाया and माया, धरती and भरती.
  • Punjabi pairs: ਰੁੱਖ and ਸੁੱਖ, ਛਾਂ and ਮਾਂ, ਹਰਿਆਲੀ and ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ਹਾਲੀ.

Make the drive count

  • Plant trees that belong to your area. Ask a local nursery or the forest department which ones do well.
  • Decide before planting who will water each sapling through the first summer.
  • Write the planting date and the carer's name on a small tag. Visit it on the same date next year.

What to avoid

  • Slogans that only count saplings planted. Count the ones that are alive a year later.
  • Nailing boards and banners into living trees.

Using these slogans

  • A plantation slogan means more with a promise: name who will water the sapling.
